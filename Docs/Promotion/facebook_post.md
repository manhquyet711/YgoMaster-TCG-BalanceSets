## Post

🃏 **Classic Yu-Gi-Oh! character decks, now playable in Master Duel (free offline mod)**

Ever wanted to grab Joey's deck and take on Kaiba, Pegasus or Grandpa's Exodia, with every deck built to be fair against the others? I made a free mod that does exactly that 👇

✨ **Three character modes in Solo**
• **Balanced Characters:** 15 old-school decks by Reddit user Rosehn (Yugi, Kaiba, Joey, Mai, Pegasus, Bakura, Jaden and more)
• **Goat Era Duelists:** 8 duelists using only 2005 Goat format cards
• **Duel Royale:** Konami's 7 official Duel Royale decks

🎮 **How it plays**
Pick a character, then beat every other one. Both decks are fixed, so it's your play skill, not your collection. The CPU plays at full strength, and both sides start at 8000 LP.

📜 **Also included:** the TCG Solo Campaign, using the real ban list from each era, from 2002 Yugi-Kaiba format up to modern formats.

📊 I let the game's own AI play every matchup (over 2,000 duels) to check the balance. The results are on GitHub.

🔒 Offline only. It doesn't connect to Konami's servers or touch your real account.
